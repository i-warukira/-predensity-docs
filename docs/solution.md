# The Solution

Instead of asking *"will this happen or not"*, Predensity asks **"where will this be, and when?"**

A trader picks a price range $$[y_1, y_2]$$ and a resolution time $$x$$. At time $$x$$, an oracle fetches the actual price. If it falls within $$[y_1, y_2]$$, the bet wins.

No market creation. No liquidity bootstrapping. No manual resolution.

**Being right is the minimum requirement.** What the system rewards is the *quality* of being right — a narrow, contrarian, early prediction is worth far more than a wide, consensus, last-minute one. Both may win. They are not equally valuable.
