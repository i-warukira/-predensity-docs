# The Solution

Instead of asking *"will this happen or not"*, Predensity asks **"where will this be, and when?"**

A trader picks a price range $$[y_1, y_2]$$ and a resolution time $$x$$. At time $$x$$, an oracle fetches the actual price $$p$$. If $$y_1 \leq p \leq y_2$$, the bet wins.

No market creation. No liquidity bootstrapping. No manual resolution.

**Being right is the minimum requirement.** What the system rewards is the *quality* of being right — scored on [sharpness and lead time](prediction-quality.md). A narrow, early prediction earns a higher weight and therefore a larger share of the [payout pool](payouts.md).
