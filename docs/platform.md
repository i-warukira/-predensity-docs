# Platform

**Currency:** USDC — stable unit of account, no currency risk on top of prediction risk.

**Access:** Sign in with email. A non-custodial wallet is created automatically. No seed phrases. No extensions. *Predensity never holds your private keys.*

**M-Pesa:** Users in East Africa can deposit KES directly, bridged to USDC — no crypto onboarding required.

**Fee:** 0.5% on all bets at placement, routed to the protocol treasury.

**Contracts:** All smart contracts are publicly verifiable on-chain.
