# The Probability Map

The platform displays a live **KDE chart** on each market — a visual distribution of where active bets are clustered across price and time.

This is a *read-only signal layer*: traders can see where the crowd is concentrated before placing their own prediction. It does not feed into the quality calculation — sharpness and lead time are the two scoring dimensions.

**What the chart shows:**
- Distribution of active bets by price range
- Stake-weighted density — larger stakes create denser regions
- Useful for spotting consensus clusters and underexplored price zones before committing

*The chart updates as new bets are placed and old ones resolve.*
