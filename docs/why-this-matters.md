# Why This Matters

The most profitable action on Predensity is also the most socially valuable one: *providing accurate, early, and precise data*.

When incentives reward *[precision](prediction-quality.md)* and *foresight* — not just correctness — the crowd produces better information than any individual analyst or model. This is the core of **Info Finance**: turning private speculation into a public signal.

The [prediction chart](probability-map.md) that emerges from Predensity's activity is a real-time, crowd-sourced density surface $$p(x)$$. It aggregates kernels from participants with different knowledge, models, and risk tolerances. The more people participate, the denser and more informative the distribution — for everyone, including those who never place a bet.

## **Against corposlop**

Vitalik Buterin coined *corposlop* to describe the low-quality, high-frequency, speculative noise that dominates prediction markets built for short-term revenue. Short-term crypto price bets and sports wagers bring in revenue during bear markets — but they produce no lasting information value.

WKP/DPM is the structural answer. Its non-discrete, precision-weighted nature makes participation valuable even for those with partial information. By rewarding near-misses proportionally and penalizing lazy wide ranges, it transforms prediction markets from a dopamine loop into a **high-resolution information infrastructure**.

*The age of blunt wagers is ending. The era of mathematical truth has begun.*
